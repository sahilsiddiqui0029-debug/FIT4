3. Legal Bases for Processing

  • Consent (DPDP Act §6): Health data processing requires and receives your explicit consent through our in-app consent flow.
  • Legitimate Interest: Security monitoring, fraud prevention, system stability.
  • Legal Obligation: Compliance with CERT-In directives, IT Act 2000, ABDM policies.

4. ABDM & Health Data Sharing

We are an ABDM-integrated Health Information User (HIU). When a doctor requests your health records:

  • You receive a push notification on your registered mobile app.
  • You must explicitly Approve or Deny the request. No data is shared without your approval.
  • Approved consents expire after 7 days by default and can be revoked at any time from the Privacy & Permissions screen.
  • Data exchanged via ABDM is formatted as FHIR R4 Observation resources per NHA specification.

5. Data Security

  • Encryption in transit: TLS 1.3 with HSTS. Certificate pinning in the mobile app.
  • Encryption at rest: AES-256-GCM field-level encryption for phone and ABHA ID. Database-level encryption on AWS RDS Mumbai (ap-south-1).
  • Access control: Phone numbers only shown to doctors with active, patient-approved ABDM consent.
  • Audit logging: Every doctor access to patient data is logged per CERT-In IS/IEC 27001 requirements.
  • Device security: The mobile app refuses to run on rooted or jailbroken devices.
  • VAPT: Annual Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing by a CERT-In empanelled auditor.

7. Data Localisation

All personal data and health data is stored exclusively within India on AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1) region. We do not transfer health data outside India.

8. Cookies & Analytics

We use first-party session cookies for authentication. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. No personal health data is ever shared with analytics platforms.

9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for persons under 18 years of age. For family member monitoring (minors), parental consent must be provided through our Family Care feature.
